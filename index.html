What's inside

Four ways to train. One membership.

Elevate was built so training, playing and recovering never means driving somewhere else.

Gym Floor

Free weights, plate-loaded machines, cable stations and a dedicated functional zone — programmed for beginners and loaded for the strong.

Sports Court

Indoor hardwood court for basketball, volleyball and pickup games. Book it for your team, your barkada or your company league.

Billiards

Tournament-grade tables for the post-session unwind. The lifestyle half of Lifestyle and Fitness — bring friends, stay a while.

Recovery

Stretch and mobility space, massage and recovery services so tomorrow's session is as strong as today's. Train hard, recover harder.

Pilates Reformer Studio

A bright, fully-equipped reformer studio running small-group sessions midweek and weekends. Low impact, seriously strong — perfect alongside your lifting or as a programme of its own. See this week's Pilates slots →
