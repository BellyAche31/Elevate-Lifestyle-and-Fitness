Who we are

More than a gym

Elevate Lifestyle and Fitness is a wellness-focused company dedicated to helping individuals achieve sustainable health, peak performance and balanced living.

What we do

By combining fitness training, recovery optimisation, lifestyle coaching and holistic wellness principles, we help members transform not only their bodies, but also their mindset, habits and long-term well-being.

Our mission

To inspire and guide individuals toward healthier, stronger and more fulfilling lives through personalised fitness programmes and lifestyle-driven wellness solutions.

Our vision

To become a trusted leader in the lifestyle and fitness industry by redefining health as a balanced integration of physical fitness, mental well-being and sustainable daily habits.

What's inside

Five ways to spend an evening here

Train, play, unwind and recover without leaving the building.

Gym Floor

Free weights, plate-loaded machines, cable stations, a dumbbell rack, cardio zone and a turfed functional area — all fully air-conditioned.

Pilates & Spinning Studio

A dedicated studio for reformer Pilates, spinning and the rest of the group class timetable. Capped sizes, proper coaching.

FIBA-Standard Court

A full basketball court built to FIBA dimensions. Book it for your team, your barkada or a company league.

Recovery, Spa & Sauna

Sauna, massage and recovery services so tomorrow's session is as strong as today's. Members get free use of the sauna, plus body composition analysis to track progress.

Billiards & Lounge

Tournament tables and a proper lounge for the post-session unwind — the lifestyle half of Lifestyle and Fitness.
